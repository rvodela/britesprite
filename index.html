Questions

 

Does coffee count toward my water goal?

Partly. Each drink counts by a simple everyday estimate — for example, coffee counts as about 80% water. You can see exactly how much each drink counted in the breakdown.

Will my buddy get thinner if I eat less?

No. If you're behind on food your buddy just looks hungry. Eating more than your goal makes it round and sleepy in a silly way — never shaming.

Can I track only water?

Yes. Turn off food tracking in Settings and your buddy will only react to drinks.

Is BriteSprite medical advice?

No. BriteSprite is a habit tracker. Goals and calorie values are everyday estimates; talk to your doctor about your own needs.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging, your buddy and the dashboard all work without an internet connection.
